Keyboard procedure

  1. Open each new page with focus in the document and press Tab.
  2. Activate “Skip to main content” with Enter.
  3. Tab through links and controls; check that every focus indicator is visible.
  4. Use Enter/Space on buttons and Enter to submit forms.
