Frequently Asked Questions

What is HTML?

HTML stands for HyperText Markup Language. It tells the web browser how to display content like text, images, and links.

What is the difference between an HTML tag and an element?

Tag is the hidden keyword inside angle brackets used to mark the start or end of content.Element is the complete combination of the opening tag, the content inside, and the closing tag.

What are HTML attributes?

Attributes provide extra information about an HTML element. They are always placed inside the opening tag and usually come in name-value pairs.

What is Semantic HTML?

Semantic HTML means choosing elements based on their meaning, not their visual appearance. It helps to shorten the code, and makes it efficient as well.
