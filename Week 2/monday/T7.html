What You Need:

  • Sour Yogurt
  • An onion
  • Some chilly
  • Chilly powder
  • Salt

Steps:

  1. Mince some onion and chilly.
  2. Pour the sour yogurt into a bowl.
  3. Put the minced onion and chilly, along with some chilly powder for color.
  4. Put salt to your taste and mix all in.
  5. Finally, serve and enjoy.
